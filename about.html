Our Story

A little corner built for slow mornings, good conversations and the comfort of a well-made cup.

HOW IT BEGAN

Born from a love of slow moments.

Mist & Mocha started with a simple idea: that a café should feel like an escape. Somewhere you can leave the rush at the door, settle into a cozy corner and let the day soften around you.

Every cup is brewed with care and every dessert is prepared fresh, because we believe small details are what turn a visit into a memory.
